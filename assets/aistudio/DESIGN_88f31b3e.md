## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural and sculptural aesthetic for an ethical aesthetic medicine institute, subverting standard spa clichés. Rejecting conventional romantic flourishes and sterile clinical tropes, the visual language synthesizes avant-garde editorial poise with medical rigor. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- Pure ivory planes acting as monolithic sculptural canvases.
- Strict rectangular geometries (0px radius) evoking structural confidence and surgical precision.
- High-contrast, expressive typography juxtaposing the expressive glyphs of Syne against the functional, calm cadence of DM Sans.
- A deliberate tension between warm mineral neutrals and disciplined chromatic accents (deep plum, gold, and vivid action magenta).

The emotional register is sober, intellectual, uncompromisingly ethical, and reassuringly serene. It caters to a discerning clientele seeking genuine individualized assessment rather than fast-track cosmetic promises.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental calm with surgical chromatic precision:

- **Primary (`#CC3366` - Vivid Magenta):** Reserved strictly for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and conversion touchpoints. Its application must be deliberate and sparing to preserve its high focal impact.
- **Secondary (`#67235E` - Deep Plum):** The architectural anchor. Applied to noble headlines, premium editorial badges, and structural full-bleed containers.
- **Tertiary (`#B88508` - Structural Gold):** Utilized for structural hairlines, monogram outlines, and understated accreditation badges.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Ivory):** The predominant background surface across the system, providing warmth and breathing room while avoiding clinical paper-white glare.
- **Semantic Named Neutrals:**
  - `tinta` (`#333333`): Primary high-contrast body text.
  - `tinta-suave` (`#494D50`): Secondary metadata, captions, and disclaimers.
  - `niebla` (`#CCD6DF`): Ghost borders, structural dividers, and cell demarcations.
  - `dorado-claro` (`#D4A73A`): Subtle hover transitions and delicate gold accents.

## Typography

Typography breaks away from traditional serif expectations by introducing **Syne** as the display and headline voice. Its dramatic, wide proportions and sculpted terminals deliver an architectural, avant-garde gravitas. 

**DM Sans** grounds the system in calm, contemporary legibility, crucial for conveying medical protocols, ethical disclosures, and clinical clarity on mobile devices.

- Display and headline styles use tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain structural density.
- Labels, legal disclaimers, and micro-headers enforce uppercase letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to create crisp typographic metadata.
- Body copy is set with a relaxed line height (`1.6`) to provide effortless reading across high-density clinical descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is rooted in a structured architectural grid:
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (conforming to a maximum container width of `1320px`), an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Extensive use of `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` between thematic blocks allows ivory spaces to function as monolithic gallery expanses.
- **Structural Partitioning:** Layouts favor solid, hairline-partitioned cells (`1px solid var(--niebla)`) reminiscent of architectural blueprints over floating elements.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, skeuomorphic volume, and frosted blurs. Depth is conveyed strictly through **tonal hierarchy, surface staging, and razor-sharp hairlines**:

- **Planar Staging:** Contrast is generated by pairing the foundational ivory plane (`#FAF7F2`) with deep plum monolithic zones (`#67235E`) and crisp pure white surface plates (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Spatial boundaries and card boundaries are established using 1px borders in `niebla` (`#CCD6DF`) or delicate `dorado` (`#B88508`).
- **Shadowless Clarity:** Interactive elements and modals exist as planar cutouts. Elevated states use crisp offset outlines or stark border color shifts (`#B88508` or `#CC3366`) without ambient or diffused shadows.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly **sharp (`0px` border-radius)**. 

Every interactive touchpoint, container, input field, and card retains exact right angles. This reinforces an uncompromising architectural posture, precision medical standards, and an avant-garde editorial presence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Background `#CC3366`, text `#FFFFFF`, 0px radius. Font: `DM Sans` medium, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.08em`. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Hover state transitions to `#B88508` smoothly over 200ms.
- **Secondary (Editorial):** Background `#67235E`, text `#FAF7F2`, 0px border-radius.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Background transparent, 1px solid `#B88508`, text `#333333`. Hover transitions to solid `#B88508` with text `#FAF7F2`.

### Cards & Service Cells
- **Structure:** Crisp rectangular boxes bordered by `1px solid #CCD6DF`. Background `#FAF7F2` or `#FFFFFF`.
- **Interaction:** On hover, the border shifts from `#CCD6DF` to `#B88508` with no elevation shift or translation, preserving the grid's stability.
- **Content:** Headline in `Syne` (`headline-sm`), service rationale in `DM Sans` (`body-sm`), followed by mandatory "Previa Valoración" metadata tags.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Style:** Rectangular fields with background `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `1px solid #CCD6DF`. Font: `DM Sans` 1rem.
- **Focus State:** 1px outline in `#CC3366` without ambient glow. Labels sit outside or flush top in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Mandatory Protocols:** 0px radius, 1px solid `#B88508`, text `#67235E`, background transparent. Padding: `0.25rem 0.625rem`. Letter-spacing `0.08em` uppercase.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Design:** Strict square frames (`16px x 16px`), 1px solid `#333333`. Active state filled with `#CC3366` or marked with a centered geometric square. No circular shapes.

### Section Dividers
- Hairline rules (`1px solid #CCD6DF`) spanning full grid widths, occasionally accented with a centered or terminal `#B88508` marker.
